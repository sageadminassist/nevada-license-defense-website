About This Practice Area

Physical therapists (PTs) and physical therapist assistants (PTAs) facing complaints, investigations, or disciplinary proceedings before the Nevada Physical Therapy Board.

Physical therapist and physical therapist assistant licenses in Nevada are governed by NRS Chapter 640 (Nevada Physical Therapy Board). All disciplinary proceedings are conducted under NRS 233B, the Nevada Administrative Procedure Act. PTs and PTAs facing complaints regarding unprofessional conduct, supervision violations, or standard-of-care issues should retain legal counsel before any Board response.

Physical Therapy Board Defense FAQ

What law governs the Nevada Physical Therapy Board?

PTs and PTAs in Nevada are licensed and disciplined under NRS Chapter 640, with contested hearings under NRS Chapter 233B.

What complaints do Nevada physical therapists face?

Billing and documentation issues, improper supervision of PTAs or aides, boundary violations, and patient injury allegations.

Should I talk to the Nevada Physical Therapy Board before I hire a lawyer?

No. Anything you say or write to the board — including an informal phone call with an investigator — can be used in the case against you. Speak with a Nevada license defense attorney first. Craig K. Perry offers a free 30–45 minute consultation at 702.893.4777.
