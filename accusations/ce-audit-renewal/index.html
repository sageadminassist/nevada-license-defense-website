Quick answer

Renewal problems seem small but can lead to public discipline in Nevada: failing a continuing-education audit, practicing on an expired license, or giving an inaccurate answer to a renewal question about arrests, discipline, or health. Boards often view an incorrect renewal answer as a false statement. These matters are usually very fixable when handled promptly and accurately.

By Craig K. Perry, Attorney at Law · Last updated November 2026 · General information, not legal advice for your specific case.

How This Allegation Shows Up

  • Missing CE certificates in a random audit
  • Practicing after the license expiration date
  • Inaccurate answers about arrests, convictions, or prior discipline
  • Unreported discipline from another state
  • Missing required one-time courses

How Nevada Boards Handle It

Boards send audit or deficiency notices with response deadlines. Unresolved audits and false answers can lead to fines, citations, or formal disciplinary complaints.

See the full Nevada board discipline process, step by step.

How We Defend These Cases

  • Respond accurately and on time with the right documents
  • Correct prior inaccurate answers the right way
  • Negotiate citations or fines instead of formal discipline

What to Do Right Now

  1. Gather every CE certificate and transcript you have
  2. Don't guess on renewal questions — get advice if unsure
  3. Stop practicing immediately if your license has lapsed

CE Audits & Renewal Issues FAQ

What happens if I fail a Nevada CE audit?

Typically the board gives you a deadline to cure. Unresolved deficiencies can lead to fines or discipline.

I answered a renewal question wrong. What now?

Correct it carefully. A well-prepared correction usually goes better than the board discovering it later.

Is practicing on an expired license a big deal?

Yes. It can be treated as unlicensed practice, so stop and address it immediately.
