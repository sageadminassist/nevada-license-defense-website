About This Practice Area

Chiropractic physicians facing complaints, investigations, or disciplinary proceedings before the Nevada Chiropractic Physicians Board.

Chiropractic physician licenses in Nevada are governed by NRS Chapter 634 (Nevada Chiropractic Physicians Board). All disciplinary proceedings are conducted under NRS 233B, the Nevada Administrative Procedure Act. Chiropractors facing complaints, scope-of-practice allegations, or license suspension before the Board should consult an experienced license defense attorney before responding to any Board communication.

Your first consultation is always free — a 30–45 minute call to review your situation. In-person and secure Zoom meetings available.
